How to report a fault

  1. Sign in and choose Report a problem, or go to Help → My support tickets.
  2. Pick how serious it is, describe what happened and add any error message.
  3. You get a reference number, an alert and an email for every update.
  4. If the portal itself is down, email {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }} and quote the time it happened.

Escalation

  1. If a response is late, the ticket is flagged red in the support queue for the whole team.
  2. Critical faults go to the on-call engineer straight away.
  3. After a critical fault, we publish a short incident report within 5 working days.
