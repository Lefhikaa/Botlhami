The innovation ecosystem

Every idea follows the same open, fair path, and you can see where yours is at every step.

  1. SubmissionDescribe your idea and upload your documents. Save a draft whenever you like.
  2. ReviewIndependent evaluators score it against six published criteria, without seeing your name.
  3. FeedbackRead their comments, answer questions, or improve it and resubmit.
  4. ApprovalCEDA's adjudication panel decides, guided by an explained recommendation.
  5. AwardFunding referral, incubation, recognition or a pilot with CEDA.

Innovators

Citizens and businesses with an idea, from a first concept to a working pilot.

Independent evaluators

Experts who score each idea separately, declare any conflict of interest and never see each other's scores.

CEDA's adjudication panel

Makes the final decision and grants awards, with every step recorded.

Regulators and partners

CIPA, BURS, BOBS, BOCRA and others, with contacts in the help centre.

Forms and documentsThe application form, templates and what to upload. Questions and contactsAnswers to common questions, and which regulator to call. Talk to CEDALive chat from 09:00 to 16:00 on working days.

Botlhami is the innovation stream for CEDA's #KaeKapaKae platform, built to help citizen innovators turn ideas into businesses.
